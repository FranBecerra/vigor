# Generator optimization review — 2026-09-30

## Method and acceptance

This is a software/programming audit, not a trial demonstrating superior hypertrophy. The weighted selector remains the production default. The 30 existing hypertrophy cases and strength control were retained. Restricted equipment, short sessions, simultaneous priorities/depriorities, empty catalogues, declared volume, selected body regions, exercise exclusions and strength at each level expand the matrix to 55 configurations. Ten seeds plus one five-minute availability perturbation produce 605 runs: 198 beginner, 209 intermediate and 198 advanced.

All 605 runs were checked automatically and have individual rows and full prescriptions in the companion artifacts. The 31 original base cases were inspected individually below; this is **not** a claim of 605 independent manual coaching reviews. The 33 empty-catalogue controls deliberately cannot produce a valid routine. The other 572 runs have no checked hard failures or crashes.

Hard checks cover empty sessions, unavailable/excluded exercises, hypertrophy identity duplication, exercise set ceilings, time overflow, unassigned work and set conservation. Soft findings cover the 12-set product floor, target deficits, missing patterns, distribution and possible overlap. Twelve sets is not a physiological minimum. A passing gate does not establish appropriate recovery, technical competence or an optimal individual dose.

## Isolated stages

1. Archived the original 129-run baseline before changes. Bounded additional feasibility probes repair a non-monotonic volume search: restricted-equipment case I increases from 63 to 67 sets without inventing a new target.
2. Extracted dose allocation from roster selection; the isolated comparison preserved outputs.
3. Expanded to 605 runs and captured a second baseline. A tiny time squeeze previously removed an exercise slot even from prioritized muscles. Protecting their existing allowance reduces the intermediate emphasis scenario's ten-seed span from 16 to 8 sets and removes its large triceps deficits.
4. Repaired redundant hip-hinge placement through feasible alternative placement and whole-exercise exchanges. Time, set totals, existing frequency and anchors remain constraints.
5. Tested supplementary descriptive catalogue tags separately. Rejected promotion: they alter novelty ranking and cause volume regressions despite reasonable descriptions. No new biological ratings or default ranking weights were invented.

The initial-comparison artifacts use the original 129-run baseline. The final artifacts use the expanded 605-run baseline **after** search repair and phase extraction but **before** priority and hinge repairs. These baselines must not be conflated.

There are no new checked hard failures, no seed spans above the gate's 10-set tolerance, no losses above three sets with five extra minutes, and no per-case volume losses beyond the gate's max(3 sets, 10%) tolerance. These thresholds are regression policy, not biological constants. The remaining 435 short sessions include deliberate six-day/30-minute stress cases; they cannot be described as satisfactory plans merely because the gate passes. Fewer-day suggestions remain warnings requiring user choice, not silent schedule changes.

**Trade-off:** undated overlap warnings increase by 15. Hinge redundancy improves, but recovery is not solved globally. The new optional dated calendar supplies actual gaps and places available rest slots preferentially between sessions sharing direct work. It cannot guarantee recovery or prescribe a universal number of required rest days.

## Which exercises win?

Among 176 nonempty hypertrophy runs per level, the most frequent beginner selections include machine lateral raise (121), cable lateral raise (106), ab wheel (102) and close-grip bench (101). Intermediate selections include close-grip bench (147), seated leg curl (142), machine lateral raise (136) and inclined lateral raise (129). Advanced selections include close-grip bench (165), ab wheel (141), machine lateral raise (136) and seated leg curl (133).

Close-grip bench in 165/176 advanced plans is a concentration warning, not evidence it is universally best. Compound bonuses, indirect credit and the eligible roster can dominate selection. Technique suitability is still weakly individualized. Of 115 auto-eligible exercises, only 14 have original explicit stimulus tags; 101 lack those descriptors. The supplementary description-derived registry marks resistance profiles and minimum experience as unverified/not established. It remains diagnostic rather than a production-ranking change.

## Calendar, duration and evidence boundaries

Workouts and rest days now have optional civil dates, separate identities and transactional persistence. Rest checkmarks do not advance a microcycle or certify recovery. Moving a workout preserves an uncompleted rest marker at its former date; occupied dates and checked rest days are protected. Existing undated routines retain session-based progression. Non-seven-day calendars show a seven-day volume equivalent without silently changing dose.

Duration separates work, rest, setup and conservative allowance. Home and generation share the model. Five comparable completed workouts enable observational median feedback; an optional bounded multiplier is calculated but not automatically applied to volume or prescriptions. Rest intervals are editable per exercise. No real-user timing dataset was available in this audit.

Stretch, stability, progressability, ROM, resistance profile and systemic-cost judgements remain expert heuristics, not measured hypertrophy or CNS-fatigue quantities. MEV/MAV/MRV inputs are starting priors until observed response supports individual adjustments. Indirect 0.5-set credit is bookkeeping, not exact equivalence. A [longitudinal cable versus dumbbell lateral-raise trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40692697/) cannot rank the entire catalogue. Calendar updates follow [Firestore transaction semantics](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/manage-data/transactions); Expo compatibility was checked against [v57 documentation](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/).

## Verification and reproduction

873 tests pass across 43 suites. TypeScript, catalogue validation (378 exercises) and iOS JavaScript/asset export pass. Export is not a signed native build or an on-device UI test. Real Firestore interaction, calendar usability on the phone and longitudinal recovery remain unverified.
