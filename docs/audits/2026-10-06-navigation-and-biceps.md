## Source/evidence boundary

Individually read guide pages: 233 bar curl, 234 hammer, 238 incline dumbbell, 239 seated bilateral Bayesian, 241 machine preacher, 243 low-cable bar curl, 244 chest-supported spider, 250 unilateral concentration. Page 239 requires a backrest/two cable stations: the generic Bayesian alias cannot inherit those stability/availability attributes. Page 241 depends on machine geometry; existing lengthened-resistance/profile assumptions need an isolated experiment. Page 238 remains a source review without merging its guide import into the original incline-curl identity. No score, stimulus tag or automatic eligibility changed.

A [10-week within-person trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40082069/) compared cable preacher/Bayesian curls with matched resistance profiles in 15 young men, finding no significant between-condition hypertrophy difference. This does not prove universal equivalence; it argues against automatic Bayesian superiority based on shoulder extension alone. Cable tension versus joint torque and support/availability are mechanical/setup inferences, not measured growth rankings.
